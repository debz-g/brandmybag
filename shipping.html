Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: 28 August 2026

BrandMyBag doesn't ship any physical product to you. What you're buying is ad placement — your logo, printed as a sticker, applied to a specific spot on a personal backpack that stays with the seller. Nothing is mailed to the buyer.

What "delivery" means here

  • After payment, you send your logo/artwork by email or X DM.
  • The sticker is produced and applied to the claimed spot on the bag, typically within 2-3 business days.
  • You'll get a photo confirmation once it's applied.

Delays

If production or application is delayed beyond a few days, you'll be notified directly with an updated timeline.
