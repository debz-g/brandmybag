How it works

Pick a spot

Five spots across the bag's front and side panels, priced by size and visibility. Preview your logo on the full bag first.

2

Pay to claim it

Claiming a spot pays the current bid via a Razorpay link. Get outbid, and whoever's next has to beat your price.

3

Your logo rides along

Printed as a sticker, applied within a couple of days, and it travels with me through Outer Ring Road traffic until someone outbids you.

FAQ

Is this real?

Yes. It's also explicitly an experiment — I'm curious whether a format like this works without an existing audience, not just for accounts that already went viral.

What happens if I get outbid?

I'll reach out about a refund path or you can immediately place a higher bid to reclaim the spot.

Are you affiliated with Arctic Fox?

No. This is an independent, unaffiliated project. Bag photos are the manufacturer's listing photos used as placeholders during setup, and will be replaced with my own photos of my own bag.

How long does a spot last?

Until someone outbids you. No fixed end date — this isn't a 14-day auction, it's ongoing.
