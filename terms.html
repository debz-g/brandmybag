What this is

BrandMyBag is an independent experiment run by an individual (Debayan Ghosh Dastider), not a registered company. It auctions physical sticker placements on a personal backpack carried daily through Outer Ring Road traffic in Bengaluru. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Arctic Fox or any bag manufacturer.

How claiming a spot works

  • Each spot shown on the site has a current price. Paying that price via the provided Razorpay link claims the spot.
  • A claimed spot is held by you until another party pays a higher amount to claim it — there is no fixed end date.
  • If you're outbid, you'll be contacted about a refund (see the Refund Policy).
  • Once a spot is claimed, a sticker with your provided logo will be produced and applied to the bag within a reasonable time, typically a few days.

What you're paying for

You're paying for physical ad placement — a printed sticker in a specific location on a personal bag, for as long as you hold that spot. This is not a guarantee of any specific number of views, impressions, or business outcomes.

Content restrictions

Logos or artwork submitted must not be illegal, hateful, sexually explicit, or infringe on someone else's trademark or copyright. Submissions that violate this may be declined and refunded.
